# FOYER brand kit

## Idea

**Foyer** is threshold into a room: a welcoming place before live conversation begins.

## Mark

Use `foyer-door-mark.png` as a standalone app/avatar/social mark. Keep clear space equal to one quarter of mark width. Do not recolour, add shadows, or place over busy imagery.

## Palette

- Ink: `#171310`
- Cream: `#F6F2EA`
- Signal: `#B7FF19`
- Moss: `#29331C`
- Smoke: `#A49C90`

Signal is an accent, never a large text colour on cream. Use Ink or Cream for long-form copy.

## Type

- Display: high-contrast serif, editorial and warm.
- Text: neutral sans-serif, compact and legible.

## Image direction

Use candid evening scenes: conversation, soft practical light, real spaces, visible texture. Avoid glossy office scenes, neon cyberpunk, staged smiles, or generic dashboard mockups.
