# EzChat rebrand

Self-authored under the user's explicit creative delegation.

- **Vibe:** nocturnal, social, editorial, immediate, human.
- **Journey:** discover live rooms, understand drop-in video chat, enter a room.
- **Energy:** quiet invitation, lively room discovery, focused commitment.
- **Feeling:** curiosity, belonging, confidence. Peak: the live-room mosaic.
- **Signature move:** the directory feels like a living broadcast board, not a SaaS dashboard.
- **Direction:** premium editorial community space; no gaming neon, glassy gradients, or generic feature-card grids.
- **Structure:** distinct scenes with a clear entry path and no invented social proof.
- **Assets:** use the existing product and brand primitives only.

Tell someone: *EzChat is the chat-and-cam site where a room already feels alive before you enter it.*
